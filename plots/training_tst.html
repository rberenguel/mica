Curriculum

PhaseStepsLayoutEnd trainEnd valDescription
Phase 1 (Superposition): Output-only MCE loss with decaying bag sizes (16→8→4→2) on a 10:1 Gutenberg:Noir mix. Learns coarse semantic space without burning data.

Phase 2 (Folding): Standard CE on pure Gutenberg with origami layer folding (*2 *2 1) to force syntax regularisation, followed by an unfold cooldown.

Phase 3 (Noir): WSD schedule on 100% Noir with optional initial blend. Model fully unfolded for maximum stylistic capacity.
