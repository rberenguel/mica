Mica

De mica en mica s'omple la pica

Mica is a tiny (5.4M parameter) Transformer trained on Project Gutenberg and fine-tuned on hardboiled noir fiction — Dashiell Hammett, Black Mask magazine, pulp detective stories. It is not meant to be useful, or even accurate. It was a fun experiment in very small models, meant to sound like a 1920s dime-novel ghost.

The model uses an "origami" cyclic training architecture: during Gutenberg pre-training, outer transformer layers are repeatedly folded (shared weights) and unfolded (restored) as a regulariser. The final model is fully unfolded for inference. A smaller variant, Cromulent Noir (2.5M parameters), was trained longer with the same curriculum to see how low a tiny model can go.

A third variant, Cromulent Noir — Student, uses a teacher-student hybrid objective: the model learns next-token prediction while also aligning its latent representations with a slowly-updating teacher network. This regulariser encourages richer, more stable contextual embeddings without increasing parameters.
